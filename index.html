Ziqi Liu 刘子琦

My name is Ziqi Liu (CN: 刘子琦). This is my personal academic website.

About

I am a doctoral student at Università degli Studi di Milano and my advisors are Laura Pertusi and Paolo Stellari. I belong to the Complex Algebraic Geometry Group. I am partly supported by the FIS grant SCoNVAG.

Research

I work on algebraic geometry and the associated homological structures.

Education

I was a master student at Rheinische Friedrich-Wilhelms-Universität Bonn and wrote my master thesis under the supervision of Daniel Huybrechts in 2023.

I was an undergraduate student at Jilin University in Changchun and wrote my bachelor thesis under the supervision of Xiaosong Sun in 2020.
